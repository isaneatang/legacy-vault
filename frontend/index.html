Legacy Vault is a dead man's switch on BOT Chain. Lock funds in a vault, prove you're alive with a periodic check-in, and if you ever stop, the people you named inherit: gradually, disputably, and without trusting anyone's word but the chain.

How it works

  1. 1

    Create a vault

    Deposit BOT and name beneficiaries with percentage shares. Pick a check-in interval (hours to years), an optional guardian, a dispute window, and how much releases first.

  2. 2

    Check in while alive

    One transaction resets your deadline. That's the entire maintenance cost: a few cents whenever the timer gets low.

  3. 3

    Silence triggers release

    Miss your deadline and anyone can trigger the vault; there is no gatekeeper. A dispute window opens where you or your guardian can cancel with one click if it was a mistake.

  4. 4

    First tranche unlocks

    After the dispute window lapses, beneficiaries claim their share of the first tranche (e.g. 25%). Every beneficiary pulls their own slice directly from the contract.

  5. 5

    Remainder follows

    One more full interval of silence and the rest unlocks. But if you reappear at any point before that, a single check-in revives the vault to full control.

Why you can trust it

Deterministic, not predictive

No AI guessing if you're dead, no oracle reporting it. The contract only counts time since your last check-in. Simple rules can't hallucinate.

Graduated, not all-or-nothing

A first partial tranche plus a second confirmation period means one forgotten deadline never empties your estate. Two strikes before everything moves.

Disputable by design

A guardian you choose, or you yourself, can cancel any trigger during the dispute window. False alarms are a one-click fix.

Pull payments

Beneficiaries claim their own slices. One broken wallet can never block the others from receiving.

No admin key

The contract has no upgrade path and no owner override. What's deployed is what runs, auditable forever on the explorer.

Your rules, any timescale

Intervals from minutes (for testing) to years (for real planning). Multiple vaults per address for different beneficiaries.

Set it up once. Check in occasionally.

Everything lives on-chain under your control until the day it matters. Start with a small test vault and a short interval to see the whole flow work end-to-end.
